|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-07 | Spoofing | `by_host` | medium | mitigate | Only exact registered domains listed in the table map to a company; aggregator sites are never listed (the planner keeps the is_aggregator_host guard of suggest.py, plan 10-17); unknown domains give None, never a guess |
