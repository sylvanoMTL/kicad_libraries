<objective>
Close CR-01, the one verification gap of Phase 10 (truth 6 FAILED). When a vendor footprint's own text names no package family, build_plan takes the family from the best official KiCad footprint. Today it accepts the first entry of rank() at score 30, and rank() orders ties alphabetically, so an ambiguous IPC-style code such as SOIC127P600X175-8N or LQFP48 writes another package's family and pin count (SOIC-20, LQFP-100) into the footprint description, the generated tags, the IPC-style name and the symbol keywords. This breaks D-28, "nothing is guessed" (plan 10-05 prohibition, plan 10-23 family truth).

The fix follows the owner decision of 2026-10-28, taken after the plan check: **tied matches supply the family only when they all agree.** rank() returns every scored footprint. The family is used when every footprint at the top score names the same family, at any score of 30 or more. A single top match is the one-entry case. This replaces closure contract (a) of 10-VERIFICATION.md (`gaps.missing`, "score >= 50, or a leading-letters-only match where exactly one footprint holds the top score") with the owner's rule. Closure contract (b) is met as written: the two-SOIC test in tests/test_generated_text.py takes no family, because its two footprints disagree.

Purpose: the generated footprint text and keywords carry only package facts that were read or computed, never one picked by a tie-break (FLD-03, FLD-01).
Output: a private helper `_agreed_family_texts` in `planner.py` with a rewritten comment, rank()'s `limit` accepting None in `footprint_index.py` (signature and one docstring line), new family tests in `tests/test_generated_text.py` (synthetic stock trees plus the installed KiCad 10 footprints), and one skip-row update in `tests/README.md`.

Human checks: no expected text of the 11 end-of-phase checks changes. Check 3 keeps "WSON-6 2.1x2.1 mm, 0.65 mm pitch, TI DQK0006B", which a new installed-KiCad test now pins.
