<objective>
Close review warning WR-02. `INTERFACE_WORDS` in `kicad_lib_manager/importer/keywords.py` maps the bare word "can" to the keyword CAN, and `_matched` tests whole words of the casefolded description. So "You can use it; USB C receptacle" yields CAN, which the planner then writes into the symbol's keywords for good. Reproduced during planning through build_plan: the CSD sample with that description gets "CSD85301Q2 Transistor FET receptacle USB-C USB CAN DQK0006B TI Texas".

The fix uses both mechanisms that the review's WR-02 fix names: matching against the original-case text, and companion phrases. CAN counts when it is written in upper case as a whole word of the original text, or when a CAN companion phrase is present in any case. SPI, UART and BLE stay as they are, by the planner's judgement, not an owner decision (see Flagged Assumptions). All-caps prose such as "YOU CAN USE IT" still gives CAN: the owner accepted this residual on 2026-10-28, and a test pins it (see Owner decision 2026-10-28). The change stays inside keywords.py and its tests.
