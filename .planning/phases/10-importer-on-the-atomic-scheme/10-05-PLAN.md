## Flagged Assumptions

- **IPC name (RESEARCH A6, Open Question 3).** "IPC name" is KiCad's IPC-derived pattern `<Family>_<W>x<H>mm_P<pitch>mm`; the IPC-7351B long name needs the body height, which a footprint file does not hold.
- **Measured body, not nominal (RESEARCH A7, Open Question 2).** The CSD sentence reads "WSON-6 2.1x2.1 mm" from the vendor's F.Fab box, not the stock nominal "2x2 mm"; the owner edits it in the preview if wanted.
- **Family only from text (RESEARCH A8).** Without a vendor descr or a matched official footprint the family is omitted.
- **Insertion after (layer) (RESEARCH A13).** Verified with kicad-cli 10.0.7 for the footprint and module roots; other roots are caught by the existing load gate.
