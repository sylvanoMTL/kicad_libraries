### WR-07 weighed against plan 10-30's must-haves

**Reading of the claim.** Confirmed by code, not only by the review. `refuse()` goes through `_refused`, which always sets `conflicts=()` and `renames=()` (`planner.py:844-845`). The footprint and model conflicts are computed before the refusal return (`:1479-1487`, return at `:1535`) and thrown away. `import_dialog._on_build_result` calls `_sync_conflicts(plan)` on every accepted-from-the-worker plan (`:2537`), and `carry_resolutions` keeps only choices whose key the plan still has (`form.py:323-`), so a chosen footprint Overwrite or Rename is dropped while the typed name passes through a refused state. The loss of a chosen resolution is not new: before 10-30 the refused plan also had `conflicts=()`.

**Does it break a must-have of 10-30? No.**

- Truth 1 (G-10-11, ATXK-H14 with an empty library) names five reasons; a fresh ATXK-H14 has no installed footprint or model to conflict with, so the five reasons are complete for the UAT scenario. Verified by the real-dialog test.
- Truth 2 says the Issues are "the package and candidate Issues, then the plan's library-independent Issues (the gate's ERRORs among them), then install.invalid_library". Conflicts are not `plan.issues`; they are a separate `conflicts` field. The literal must-have holds.
- Truth 3 and the prohibition (no file operation for a refused name) are unaffected, and in fact the review's proposed fix would not add a path either.

**What it does contradict:** the prose of the plan objective, the `build_plan` docstring ("listing every library-independent reason before the refusal") and the SUMMARY claim completeness, and the UAT gap wording is "every reason Install is blocked at once". For the case "a differing footprint or 3D model is already installed and the name is refused" that is an overstatement: the strip omits `Choose Overwrite, Rename or Skip for footprint ...` until the name is fixed. This does not weaken the phase goal: Install stays blocked, nothing is written, and the atomic rule gate is unaffected.

**Classification: WARNING** (not a BLOCKER). It is surfaced as an owner decision in the human verification list (keep, or a small gap-closure plan that applies the review's fix). If the owner reads G-10-11 as covering conflicts, WR-07 becomes a gap and the status becomes gaps_found; I judge the pinned truths met, so I do not set that.
