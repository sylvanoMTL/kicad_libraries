Land the two pieces of Phase 2 groundwork that depend on nothing else in the phase: the Phase 1 code-review fixes in `fsutil` that the table and config writers rely on, and the deletion of the legacy CLI.

Both later consumers of fsutil (plans 02-03 and 02-04) depend on this plan, so they see one fsutil contract.
Output: updated `fsutil.py` and `tests/test_fsutil.py`, deleted CLI, updated `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md` and the REQUIREMENTS.md Out of Scope row.
