<objective>
Close UAT gap G-12-11. The owner reported, on the Importing Parts page: "The help should have numbered items, and titles should also be in the menu on the left." Root cause (12-UAT.md): `TOC_STRUCTURE` is a hand-written two-level list of groups and pages. The `<h3>` sections inside each page have no anchors and are not in the menu, `_on_topic_selected` only loads whole pages, and nothing numbers anything.

This plan derives a third menu level from each page's `<h3>` headings and gives each heading an anchor. Clicking a section scrolls to its heading. Groups, pages and sections are numbered from their menu order (1, 4.4, 4.4.7). The same number shows in the menu and in the page heading, computed when the guide is built. Every existing way of opening the guide keeps working, and no heading changes its look (UI-01).

Purpose: the owner wants to find a section from the menu and see where they are in the guide.
Output: changes to `help_dialog.py`, new tests in `tests/test_help_dialog.py`, a sturdier click in `tests/test_import_dialog.py`, and the gap-closure suite gate for 12-13 and 12-14.

## Owner input (binding)

- G-12-11 (UAT test 11, 2026-11-04): the help should have numbered items, and its titles should be in the menu on the left. Every heading carries a number that matches its place in the menu (for example 4.4 Importing Parts, 4.4.8 Conflicts). The sections of a page show in the menu under that page, and clicking one scrolls the page to that heading.
- The gap's missing list asks for four things. (1) Section entries derived from each page's `<h3>` headings, one source of truth, as a third menu level with an anchor per heading, scrolled to with `QTextBrowser.scrollToAnchor`. (2) Numbers computed from menu order at render time, the same in the menu label and the page heading, never typed into the HTML. (3) `show_topic(key)` keeps working for page keys, and a section key is optional. (4) Tests.
- Standing rules: theme tokens only, with no colour literal and no px font size (UI-01). Plain words in user-facing text.
- The owner's "4.4.8 Conflicts" is an example. With today's page order Conflicts is the seventh section of Importing Parts, so it is numbered 4.4.7. The tests assert numbers derived from the order, never a typed list of numbers.

## Choices made here (planner's discretion)

- **Number format.** The number, one space, then the label: "1 Overview", "4.4 Importing Parts", "4.4.7 Conflicts". No trailing dot, as in the owner's example. Groups without a page of their own ("2 Getting Started") are numbered in the menu only.
- **Section keys.** A section's key is `<page key>#<anchor>`, for example `importing#conflicts`. The anchor is the heading text in lower case, with every run of other characters turned into one hyphen and the ends trimmed ("Install and result" becomes `install-and-result`). Anchors are unique within a page; a test guards that. Page keys are unchanged, so every current caller and test that names a page keeps working.
- **Anchor markup** (checked in an offscreen probe, 2026-11-04). The heading becomes the h3 tag, then an empty named anchor, then the number and the original heading text, then the closing tag. Qt attaches the anchor name to the first character, `scrollToAnchor` reaches it, and the heading keeps its size and colour. Wrapping the heading text inside the anchor instead turned the heading the link colour, because of the stylesheet's `a` rule. `scrollToAnchor` works right after `setHtml`. On a hidden browser it applies when the browser is shown.
- **The menu follows the page.** The page shown, or the page of the section shown, has its sections open, and every other page's sections are folded. Group entries stay open, as today. The menu then stays short: the import window's Help opens on Importing Parts with its 10 sections open, 26 rows in all, and the other pages' sections are one click on the arrow away. If the owner prefers every page open, that is a one-line change after UAT.
- **The sources stay as they are.** `HELP_PAGES` and `TOC_STRUCTURE` keep their unnumbered text, so the many tests that read the raw pages (`_section("Start")`, "<h3>Commit</h3>" and the rest) keep working. Only `render_page(key)`, which the dialog shows, carries numbers and anchors.
