| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-09 | Denial of Service | oversized or decompression-bomb marker member | medium | mitigate | The member already passed `_prescan` (declared size, total, ratio); read at most MAX_MARKER_BYTES + 1 bytes, at most MAX_MARKERS members, skip with INFO beyond the cap; test_marker_over_cap_skipped, test_marker_limits |
| T-07-10 | Tampering | a marker written to disk or used as a path | high | mitigate | Markers are bytes keyed by name, nothing is created from them, STAGE_KINDS unchanged; test_markers_held_in_memory_only lists the staging folder |
| T-07-11 | Denial of Service | a corrupt marker member turning into a refusal or an exception | medium | mitigate | Reads are wrapped in `_READ_ERRORS` and become INFO "intake.marker_skipped"; marker_text decodes with errors="replace"; test_marker_corrupt_member_skipped, test_marker_text_shallowest |
